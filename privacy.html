Privacy notice

When submissions are enabled and you submit a fit request, the site operator receives your machine model, existing part number, OEM / compatible preference, email, optional notes, the requested SKU and the page URL. The backend records a timestamp and fields for tracking the request.

Purpose and storage

These details are used to review machine fit and availability and to reply about your inquiry. Requests are stored in the operator’s Google Sheets lead ledger and sent to the operator’s notification inbox through Gmail. No customer email is sent automatically.

The page URL sent by this form excludes query parameters and fragments. The form does not ask for payment information. Please do not include sensitive information in notes.

Technical processing

This site has no added analytics or advertising trackers and does not store form details in browser storage. Hosting and Google services may process technical request information under their own policies. A disabled form does not send a lead.

Retention and contact

A retention period and dedicated privacy contact have not yet been specified. If the operator has replied to your inquiry, you can reply to that message to ask about access, correction or deletion.

Submitting a request does not confirm compatibility, create a quote, reserve stock or place an order.
